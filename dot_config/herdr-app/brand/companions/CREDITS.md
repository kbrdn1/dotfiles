The two companions are original characters, drawn for this setup by `src/build.py` in the
style the portfolio's cameos use (see `STYLE.md`). They are not reproductions of any program's
mascot, and no pixel of a third-party asset is copied.

| Program | Companion | What it takes from the program | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `claude` | **Ember**, a round coal with a cream gem on its head | the warm orange of the site's palette (`#d4825d`) | MIT (the dotfiles' `LICENCE`) |

| every other program | its logo | the icon Herdr ships in `icons/<key>.svg`, referenced, not copied | Simple Icons CC0 1.0, LobeHub MIT, Lucide ISC (see `icons/LICENSES.md`) |

mark, sparkles) are original too, and so are the ten variants' coiffes (crown, cap, bow, sprout,
wizard's hat, beanie, halo, top hat, bird, flower), laid on a logo's tile, never on the logo itself. The palette and the painting rules come from the author's own
portfolio, `kbrdn.dev-v2` (`app/utils/cameos/art.ts`).

redistributed, and neither the script nor its check contains a pixel of him. Without them the runner shows
Ember. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
